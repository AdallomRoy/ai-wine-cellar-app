Cellar turns your wine cellar into a map you can tap, so you always know which bottle is where, and so does everyone you share it with.

MAP YOUR CELLAR
• Photograph your racks or import your cellar's plans as a PDF, and AI lays out every rack, shelf, case bay and magnum cubby, with every position labeled.
• See it as a clear slot-by-slot grid, or walk through it in 3D.

ADD BOTTLES IN SECONDS
• Tap an empty slot and snap the label: AI recognizes the producer, vintage, country, region, appellation and grape blend.
• Or pick a wine you already own, or type it in.
• Critic scores are looked up as you store the bottle.

FIND ANY WINE
• Search by name, producer, region, country, grape or critic.
• Filter by country, region, appellation, producer, vintage, grape, critic and minimum score.
• A wine kept in several slots shows once, with all of its locations, and you can take a bottle out of any of them.

SHARE YOUR CELLAR
• Invite family or friends through iCloud. Everyone can store and take bottles, and changes sync to everyone automatically.
• The activity log shows who stored or took each bottle, and when.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your cellar is kept on your devices and in your iCloud account.

BRING YOUR OWN AI KEY
Cellar's AI features use your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI or Google (Gemini), and the provider bills that usage to you. Photos you choose to analyze are sent directly to that provider. Critic scores come from the AI provider and may be incomplete or inexact.

Cellar is intended for adults of legal drinking age. Please enjoy wine responsibly.
