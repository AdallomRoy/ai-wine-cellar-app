Cellar Support

Need help? Open an issue in our Github project and we'll get back to you.

Getting started

  1. Create an API key with one AI provider: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google AI Studio.
  2. Enter it during setup, or later in Settings → Manage API Keys.
  3. Add a cellar from a photo of your racks or a PDF of your cellar's plans, and Cellar lays it out for you.
  4. Tap an empty slot to store a bottle: snap the label, pick a wine you own, or type it in.

Questions

Why do I need my own API key?

Cellar's AI features run on the provider you choose, and the provider bills that usage to your account, so you stay in control of the cost. Your key stays on your device.

How do I share a cellar?

Open the cellar, tap ⋯ and choose Share Cellar, then invite people with Messages, Mail or a link. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. When they open the invitation, the cellar appears in their app.

How quickly do changes reach everyone?

Usually within seconds when everyone is online. If someone's app is closed, they'll see the changes the next time they open it.

A shared cellar isn't showing up.

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in the Settings app and connected to the internet, then open Cellar again. The first sync of a large cellar can take a minute.

How do I leave a cellar someone shared with me?

In the cellar list, swipe left on the cellar and tap Leave. The owner's cellar isn't affected.

Where do critic scores come from?

They're looked up by your AI provider and may be incomplete or inexact. Tap Refresh Scores on a wine to look them up again.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a cellar by swiping left on it. To remove all of Cellar's iCloud data, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Cellar.
