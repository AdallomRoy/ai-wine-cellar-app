Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Cellar is built so that we, its developer, never receive your data. There are no accounts to create, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. This policy explains where your information goes when you use the app.

What you keep in Cellar

Your cellars, wines, bottle locations, activity history and the name you enter in Settings are stored on your device and synced through Apple's iCloud to your private iCloud database, so they're available on all your devices. We can't access them. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

Sharing a cellar

When you share a cellar, the people you invite can see and change it, including its wines, bottle locations and activity history with the names people entered. Apple's iCloud delivers the shared cellar to them. You can stop sharing at any time, and people you invited can leave a shared cellar whenever they like.

AI features

When you analyze a cellar or identify a bottle, the photo or PDF you choose is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected in Settings: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. When critic scores are looked up, the wine's name, producer and vintage are sent. These requests use your own API key, so they're part of your account with that provider and are handled under its terms and privacy policy:

  • Anthropic privacy policy
  • OpenAI privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

We don't receive these requests or the results.

Your API keys

API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only. They aren't synced to iCloud and are never sent to us; each key is sent only to the provider it belongs to. You can remove a key in Settings → Manage API Keys.

Exporting to Google Sheets

If you export to Google Sheets, Cellar asks you to sign in to your Google account and allow it to create files in your Google Drive. It can see and change only the spreadsheets it creates, not the rest of your Drive. Your cellars and the bottles in them (sections, bottle locations, wines, critic scores and who stored each bottle) are sent directly from your device to Google and saved as a new spreadsheet in your Drive, under Google's privacy policy. The sign-in is kept in the iOS Keychain on your device and is never sent to us; you can remove it in Settings → Disconnect Google Account, or remove Cellar's access from your Google Account. Cellar's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Camera and photos

Cellar uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo, and only the photo you pick is used.

What we collect

Nothing. Cellar contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting a cellar in the app removes it, with its wines and history, from your devices and iCloud. Deleting a shared cellar you own removes it for everyone; leaving a cellar shared with you removes only your copy. To remove all of Cellar's iCloud data, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Cellar.

Children

Cellar is about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
